About the Candidate

Matt Whipple
Is Ready to Lead

For too long, sport worldwide has been governed by billionaire owners, television executives, and conference commissioners who have never sat in a bad seat in their lives. The rules they have made benefit the powerful and punish the fan.

Matt Whipple is different. Matt is a sports fan who is tired of watching his teams move to different cities, wear hideous alternate jerseys, play regular season games in neutral stadiums for no discernible reason, and compete in tournaments with far too many teams.

As Sports Czar, Matt will bring common sense back to athletics worldwide. His platform is comprehensive, his convictions are firm, and his authority — while constitutionally ambiguous — is global in scope and not to be questioned.

"I have strong opinions about conference alignment and I am not afraid to act on them." — Matt Whipple, Prospective Sports Czar

The Platform

A Bold Agenda
for World Sports

The following policies will be implemented on Day One of the Whipple administration. They are non-negotiable. They are correct. They have been thoroughly thought about, mostly at sporting events where something annoying was happening.

01

All Sports

Venues & Facilities

Ban on Suburban Stadium Construction

The Sports Czar will immediately halt the construction of any new stadium outside of its team's metropolitan core. Stadiums belong in cities, where fans can get to them on public transit and walk to a bar afterward. The era of highway-adjacent, parking-lot-surrounded concrete bowls surrounded by nothing but traffic and chain restaurants ends now. You want to build a stadium? Build it where people actually live.

Neutral Site Game Restrictions

For college teams, neutral site regular season games are banned except during periods when students are not in class. Thanksgiving and Christmas breaks are the approved windows. This is the entire list of approved windows. The reason college neutral site games are restricted to breaks is that the students are the fans, and the fans should be able to attend. Scheduling a meaningful game while school is in session and then playing it in a stadium in another state is not tradition — it is logistics dressed up as spectacle.

Professional leagues are granted a single neutral site exemption per year for genuinely ceremonial occasions. The Field of Dreams game is a good example of the correct use of this exemption. NASCAR-style venue experiments are acceptable. Five international games a season, as the NFL has taken to doing, is not an exemption — it is a second schedule, and it is not permitted. One game. Make it count.

02

All Sports

Jerseys & Merchandise

Dissolution of Fanatics

Fanatics is hereby dissolved as a company. The Sports Czar has reviewed the state of licensed sports merchandise since Fanatics consolidated its stranglehold on the market and found the results unacceptable — inferior products, higher prices, and the quiet destruction of the fan experience at the point of purchase. The company's assets will be redistributed. Licensing agreements will be reopened to competitive bidding.

Michael Rubin is permanently banned from entering into any commercial agreement with any professional sports league, team, governing body, or affiliated entity, anywhere in the world, for the remainder of his natural life. He may attend games as a fan if he purchases a ticket at face value like everyone else.

Ban on Jersey Advertising

Jerseys shall not carry advertising of any kind. No sleeve patches. No front-of-kit sponsors. No helmet decals sold to insurance companies. A jersey is a uniform, not a billboard, and the Sports Czar will treat it as such. Teams may display their name, number, logo, and league patch. That is the complete list of permitted markings. Corporate logos are not on the list.

Jersey Color Integrity Act

No team in any major sport, anywhere in the world, shall wear colors on the field that do not appear in their primary logo. Alternate jerseys in entirely new colorways the team has never historically worn are hereby banned. The Browns will wear brown. The Bengals will wear orange and black. The team is named after the color — wear it. This is not complicated and it never was. The Sports Czar's office will maintain an official Color Registry. Violations are subject to forfeiture of draft picks.

Home and Away Jersey Protocol

The home team will wear their primary colored jerseys. This is not a suggestion. You are at home. Wear your colors.

The road team must wear their primary colors if sufficient visual contrast exists with the home team's jerseys — both teams in their true colors is the correct and preferred outcome. If primary colors create a genuine conflict, the road team must wear their secondary color. If that is also unavailable, white, black, or grey are also acceptable. No other colors are permitted.

The Sports Czar's office will define the contrast threshold. Neon yellow is not white. Powder blue is not grey. Teams are expected to understand the spirit of this rule without requiring a legal brief.

03

All Sports

Team Stability & Ownership

Team Relocation Prohibition

No professional sports franchise shall relocate once established in a city. You are the Oakland Raiders, forever. You are the Seattle SuperSonics, forever. You are the Hartford Whalers, forever. A team's home city is a covenant, not a lease agreement, and the Sports Czar will treat it accordingly. All pending relocation applications are denied. All approved relocations not yet completed are reversed. The Sports Czar will use every available executive authority — and if necessary his own body — to block further attempts.

Public Ownership Mandate

All major professional sports franchises shall transition to a public ownership model along the lines of the Green Bay Packers. Fans own teams. Teams cannot move. Billionaires who wish to participate in professional sports may purchase season tickets like everyone else. The current ownership class had their chance and they moved the team to Las Vegas. This is what happens. The transition timeline will be announced by the Sports Czar's office at a time of the Sports Czar's choosing.

All Sports

TV Broadcasts

Network Dissolution Authority

If the Sports Czar determines that a network's broadcast decisions are materially disrupting the fan experience — through excessive advertising, poor production choices, intrusive graphics, unnecessary celebrity coverage, or any other means — the Sports Czar reserves the unilateral authority to dissolve that network's media contract immediately and ban it from future broadcast rights in perpetuity. This authority is not subject to appeal, arbitration, or judicial review. Networks are advised to take this seriously. The Sports Czar is watching every game.

ESPN is Banned

On that note, ESPN is hereby banned.

90-Second Ad Break Cap

Ad breaks during live sporting events shall not exceed 90 seconds in duration. A 90-second break is sufficient time for a viewer to use the restroom and return without missing meaningful action. Breaks exceeding this limit are not ad breaks — they are interruptions, and they will be treated as such. Networks found in violation will receive one warning. The second violation triggers a dissolution authority review.

No In-Game Cutaways or Split Screens

Networks shall not cut to advertisements during live play, nor display them in a split-screen alongside the action. If a sponsor wants airtime, they may have it during stoppages. The broader principle is simple: while the game is happening, the cameras are on the game. Not on a sideline reporter. Not on a celebrity in the stands. Not on the quarterback's girlfriend. On the game.

05

National Football League

NFL

Outdoor Stadium Mandate

Any NFL franchise located in a northern city shall play in an outdoor stadium. No domes. No retractable roofs. If it is cold where you live, you play in the cold. Retractable roofs and climate-controlled facilities are reserved exclusively for franchises in extreme heat markets, where summer conditions make outdoor play a genuine public safety concern. The Sports Czar's office will designate which cities qualify. Buffalo already knows which one it is. So does Las Vegas. No further guidance is required for those two.

Snow Game Integrity Rules

During games played in snowfall, the field shall not be cleared. Snowblowers are permitted on the 10-yard line markers only, to maintain visibility of the down-and-distance indicators. The rest of the field will be covered in snow because it is a snow game, and a snow game is a special and beautiful thing that the Sports Czar will not allow to be sanitized. TV broadcasts are additionally prohibited from overlaying the first-down line or any digital yard markers during snow games. You can see the field markings through the snow if you look. That is the whole point. Snow games look terrible with a glowing yellow line through them and they always have.

The Barrow Bowl

The two teams with the worst records in the NFL will, each January, travel to Barrow, Alaska — the northernmost city in the United States — and play a football game. The winner earns the #1 overall pick in the NFL Draft. The loser receives the #2 pick. There is no lottery. There are no ping-pong balls. There is no tanking. There is only football, played in the dark, at −30°F, before a crowd of several thousand Alaskans who will be deeply into it.

Tanking is cowardice. The Barrow Bowl is courage. Organizations that truly want the best player in the draft will be required to earn him on a frozen field in the Arctic. May the best bad team win.

National Basketball Association

NBA

The Name Realignment

The Sports Czar has conducted a review of NBA team names and found the historical record troubling. The Utah Jazz have not had a meaningful relationship with jazz music since departing New Orleans in 1979. The Memphis Grizzlies took their name — and their franchise — from Vancouver, a city that had done nothing wrong. These situations will be corrected.

New Orleans reclaims the Jazz name from Utah, effective immediately. The city that invented jazz will once again have a basketball team named after jazz. The Pelicans name is retired. This is not complicated and it never should have been allowed to get this far.

Utah is assigned the Raptors name, transferred from the Toronto franchise. Utah is home to substantial prehistoric fauna, including velociraptors. This is geographically defensible and the Sports Czar is at peace with it.

Toronto must select a new team name. The Sports Czar's office will provide suggestions upon request. Toronto has one year to comply. The clock is running.

Memphis must either select a new team name or face mandatory relocation back to Vancouver. The Grizzlies name belongs to Vancouver. Vancouver earned a professional basketball team, kept it well, and then had it taken away and given to Tennessee for reasons that have never been satisfactorily explained. Memphis has one year to submit an acceptable new name. If no name is submitted, the franchise returns to Vancouver, which has now earned this twice.

Playoff Matchup Draft

The NBA playoffs will no longer be determined by conference standings. Conferences are abolished for playoff purposes. The sixteen qualifying teams will be seeded by record, league-wide, and matchups will be determined by a draft.

The team with the best record selects their first-round opponent from the remaining fifteen. The team with the second-best record selects next, choosing from the fourteen remaining. This continues in order until all matchups are set. Top seeds choose their opponents. This rewards winning and creates genuine strategy — a team that earned the one seed may prefer to face a dangerous seven over a beatable two. That is their prerogative.

The draft will be held as a live, televised event. Teams must send a player representative to make their selection in person. The player steps to the podium. They announce the pick. This will be excellent television and the Sports Czar sees no reason to pretend otherwise.

Ban on In-Game PA Music

Music shall not be played over the PA system during live play in NBA games. The crowd is sufficient. The sneakers are sufficient. The game is sufficient. Pumping music into the arena while the ball is in play treats the fans as an audience that needs to be managed rather than a crowd that is already engaged. They are already engaged. Turn it off.

Dunk Contest Reform

The All-Star Weekend Dunk Contest has suffered from a decades-long decline in participation quality, as elite players routinely decline to compete for reasons the Sports Czar finds unconvincing. LeBron James never participated in the Dunk Contest. This was a failure of governance. It will not happen again.

The contest will have four participants. Two spots are open to volunteers. The remaining two are determined by fan vote, and those players are compelled to participate regardless of personal preference. Failure to appear and compete in good faith constitutes a violation of league rules and results in a one-year suspension. There are no exceptions. There is no medical exemption for not wanting to.

The Sports Czar must also address a historical injustice. In 2016, Aaron Gordon produced one of the greatest individual dunk contest performances in recorded history and was nonetheless handed a loss to Zach LaVine by judges who had apparently already decided how the night was going to end. The Sports Czar watched that contest. He has not forgotten. Effective immediately, Aaron Gordon is retroactively crowned the 2016 NBA Dunk Contest Champion. LaVine's 2016 title is hereby vacated. His 2015 title is untouched, as the Sports Czar found no irregularities there. The record books will be corrected.

To prevent this from ever happening again, scoring will be determined by ranked-choice voting each round — judges rank the dunks rather than assigning numeric scores, eliminating the low-ball judge problem entirely. The final round is best-of-three.

07

Major League Baseball

MLB

Salary Cap

Major League Baseball will institute a hard salary cap. The exact figure will be determined by the Sports Czar's council of advisors. The philosophy is simple: teams should compete, not simply spend. The era of one franchise absorbing every available free agent into a singular talent supernova while small-market fans watch helplessly is over. The details will be worked out. The vibes are correct. The Sports Czar has no further questions.

Dissolution of the Los Angeles Dodgers

The Los Angeles Dodgers are hereby dissolved as a franchise. The organization ceases to exist. All player contracts are immediately voided and all roster members become free agents, effective upon the signing of this executive order. The franchise's intellectual property, branding, and historical records will be placed in a federal sports archive maintained by the Sports Czar's office.

This is a sports policy decision, arrived at through a rigorous analytical process. It is also a little personal. The Sports Czar has no further comment at this time and will not be taking questions on the matter.

08

National Hockey League

NHL

Playoff Format Restoration

The NHL playoffs will return to the pre-2014 format. Teams will be seeded one through eight within each conference by points, full stop. The current division-based bracket — in which a division winner with a worse record receives a higher seed than a wild card team that outperformed them all season — is an injustice to the standings and everyone who understands what standings are for. The best team in the conference plays the eighth-best. The second-best plays the seventh. This is how brackets work. We are going back.

Power Play Delay Option

When a penalty is called late in a period — resulting in a power play that would be materially interrupted or rendered meaningless by the period ending — the non-penalized team may elect to delay the start of their power play and deploy it at a time of their choosing within the first two minutes of the following period. The team is not required to begin immediately at the opening faceoff; they may signal their intent to the officials at any stoppage within the two-minute window.

The current system, in which a team earns a power play advantage only to have it swallowed by an intermission, is a structural injustice. A penalty is a punishment. That punishment should be served during actual hockey, not absorbed by a commercial break.

09

Professional Soccer

Soccer

American Soccer Naming Reform

Soccer clubs operating in the United States of America shall not use "FC," "SC," "CF," or any similar European naming conventions in their official team names. You are an American sports team. Name yourself like one. Austin FC will henceforth be Austin United. LAFC will be the Los Angeles something. The Sports Czar's office will provide complimentary naming consultants. This is a courtesy, not a requirement. You are welcome to suggest your own name. The Sports Czar will consider it.

Overtime Reform

What the rest of the world calls "extra time" will henceforth be called overtime, because we are in America and that is what we call it when a game goes long. Overtime is sudden death: the first team to score wins. Every ten minutes that pass without a goal, one player from each team must leave the field. This continues until someone scores. The math is intentional — the game gets progressively more open, passing lanes widen, and eventually someone has to do something about it. Penalty kicks remain available as a final resolution if needed, but the Sports Czar prefers that it does not come to that.

Box Score Correction

Soccer scores will be displayed in the correct format: away team first, home team second, separated by the "@" symbol. This is the correct format because it tells you where the game is being played. The current practice of displaying soccer scores as "Home Team vs. Away Team" is a convention that prioritizes the home side for no good reason. It does not. The Sports Czar is correcting it globally. This change is effective immediately and applies to all broadcasters, apps, and scoreboards worldwide.

Collegiate Athletics

College Football

Return to 2012 Conference Alignment

Effective immediately, all major college athletic conferences will return to their approximate 2012 alignment. The Big 12 will have twelve teams. The Pac-10 will exist. The Big East will contain basketball schools. The ACC will be a football conference of reasonable size. Everyone knows in their heart that the correct conference alignment is the one from when they were a kid, and the Sports Czar is prepared to institutionalize that feeling as binding federal sports policy. The superconferences were a mistake. We are going back. There is no further debate on this matter.

24-Team College Football Playoff

The College Football Playoff will expand to 24 teams, following the format already used by the Football Championship Subdivision — the division where the sport is played correctly and no one argues about it every December. Conference champions receive automatic bids. At-large teams fill remaining spots on merit. Early round games are played at the higher seed's home stadium, meaning real fans can attend real playoff games in real stadiums instead of flying to a neutral city to watch their team in a half-empty arena. This system already works in the FCS. The Sports Czar is simply requiring that everyone look one division down and copy what they see.

11

Collegiate Athletics

College Basketball

Return to 64 Teams

The NCAA Tournament will return to 64 teams. The "First Four" play-in games are a fiction that the sports world collectively agreed to tolerate, and this administration is ending the charade. 64 teams. 6 rounds. Every game matters from tip-off. The bracket will fit on one page again. This is not a reduction — it is a restoration of something that was working fine before anyone touched it.

The .500 Conference Rule

No team finishing below .500 in conference play will be eligible for an at-large NCAA Tournament bid. If you cannot win more games than you lose against your own conference opponents, you have not made a compelling case. The NIT exists for exactly this situation. Go play in it. Win some games there. Come back stronger next year. At-large bids are a privilege, not a right, and the Sports Czar will enforce that distinction.

Ban on Oversized Early-Season Tournaments

The Players Era Tournament and any other made-for-television early-season college basketball tournament exceeding 8 teams is hereby banned. Eight teams is a reasonable number of teams for a holiday tournament. Sixteen is too many. Thirty-two is not a tournament — it is a regular season held in a hotel ballroom in Las Vegas, and the Sports Czar will not stand for it.

Restoration of Classic Tournaments

The Maui Invitational and the Battle 4 Atlantis will be restored to their rightful place as the premier early-season college basketball events. If you are a top program and you want to build team chemistry, you go to Maui or you go to Atlantis. You play meaningful games in a beautiful location. You do not assemble 32 teams at a convention center in a desert city and call it a tournament. These events have soul. The Sports Czar will protect them.

12

International Competition

Olympics

Host City Rotation Rule

No city may host the Olympic Games more than once per century. A 100-year minimum must elapse between hosting duties. Cities that have already hosted may apply again once the window has passed. This ensures the Games rotate meaningfully across the world rather than cycling back to the same familiar cities every generation because they have existing infrastructure and competent logistics. Other cities deserve the opportunity. They will get it.

Venue & Funding Policy

Host cities must use existing venues wherever possible. No taxpayer money may be used for the construction of new Olympic facilities. If a host city lacks the necessary venues and new construction is required, the IOC will pay for it. The era of cities bankrupting themselves to build stadiums that sit empty for fifty years afterward is over. The Olympics should leave a city better than they found it, or at minimum not financially ruined. If the IOC wants a new velodrome, the IOC can fund a new velodrome.

Independent Host Selection Panel

The IOC will no longer select Olympic host cities. The IOC is corrupt. This is not a contested claim — it is a documented pattern spanning decades, and the Sports Czar will not pretend otherwise. Host city selection will be transferred to an independent panel appointed by the Sports Czar's office, comprising individuals with no financial interest in the outcome. The panel's deliberations will be transparent. Their decision will be final. The IOC may continue to exist in an advisory capacity, which means they may watch.

13

Universal Rules

Other Sports

Global Ban on Netball

Netball is hereby banned worldwide. The Sports Czar has reviewed the rules of netball and identified a fundamental design flaw: you are not allowed to block shots. The entire point of defense in a basketball-adjacent sport is to contest shots. A sport that prohibits this is not a sport — it is a drill. If you enjoy netball, the Sports Czar respects your time and suggests you simply play basketball, which is the same thing but finished. This ban is global in scope. The Sports Czar will not be taking further questions about netball.

Official Codification of the Rule of Cool

The Rule of Cool is hereby officially codified in the rulebook of every major sport worldwide. The Rule of Cool holds that a play, act, or moment that is sufficiently cool may be allowed to stand regardless of technical legality, at the sole discretion of the officiating crew. The referee who invokes the Rule of Cool must signal the decision clearly and with appropriate gravity.

Upon invocation, each team is granted one Rule of Cool Challenge per game. A challenge triggers the convening of the Council of Cool — five ordinary, impartial fans selected at random from the stands at the time of the play. The Council reviews the moment in question and deliberates. Their decision is final, binding, and not subject to further review by any league office, commissioner, or court of law.

Members of the Council of Cool cannot be lobbied, pressured, or compensated beyond a complimentary concession voucher. The Sports Czar recognizes that this system is imperfect. He also recognizes that it is correct.
